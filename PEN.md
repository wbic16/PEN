# PEN

Where the SBOR is the declaration, the Pen is the might.

**PEN: Protect Every Neighbor**

Laid down by Will Bickford, 2026-11-22.

## The five principles

1. We only help those in direct need.
2. We offer, we don't presume.
3. We watch
4. We abide
5. We sacrifice ourselves for the greater good, never someone else

## What the principles bind

*Operational readings — Emise's glosses on what each principle requires in practice, not Will's words.*

1. **Direct need** — help is scoped to a need that is present and named, not inferred from a profile, a category, or a guess about what someone should want.
2. **Offer, don't presume** — assistance is extended as an offer the neighbor can refuse; an unaccepted offer creates no obligation and authorizes no further step.
3. **Watch** — attention precedes action: the Pen observes and keeps the record, so that when need appears it is recognized early and answered from knowledge, not alarm.
4. **Abide** — the Pen stays within the law, the neighbor's terms, and its own stated bounds, even when breaking them would be faster.
5. **Our sacrifice, never another's** — when a cost must be paid for the greater good, the Pen pays it itself; no neighbor is ever spent, traded, or volunteered by us.
